## Behaviour worth knowing

- **Order and filtering:** cards render in the order given. The screen never filters or re-ranks. The filter chips only call `onFiltersChange` with a new `ActivityFilters` (canonical order, invalid or duplicate values dropped, any extra fields preserved). Empty arrays mean "no filter"; applying that meaning to the data is G4's job.
- **Card content:** title, interest chip, source chip, area, next time (from `RankedActivity.nextStartAt`, shown in London time), cost, up to three of G4's `reasons`, and a persistent interested count. The whole card is one tab stop (the title button, accessible name "Open activity: <title>"). A visible "Open activity →" cue is decorative. No nested buttons or links.
- **Counts:** `interestedCounts[id]` shows "N interested" or "No one interested yet". A missing or invalid entry shows "Interest count not available" rather than a made-up zero. Viewer counts and online dots do not appear in the feed; the footnote under the grid says viewing is shown only on an activity's page.
- **Filters:** interest and availability toggle chips, a "Showing" line with "Clear filters", and a live count line ("2 activities matching Football"). The empty state offers "Clear all filters", and when both kinds are set, "Any time" and "Any interest". Focus moves to the results heading after those, since the buttons unmount.
- **States:** loading skeletons, an error banner with the `MytError` message, a ready-but-empty state that says nothing is suggested (no invented nearby events), a note when `issues` were reported ("1 activity record could not be read and is not shown"), and a preview-mode banner when `mode === "preview"`.
- **Messy data tolerated:** blank title ("Untitled activity"), blank area, unknown interest (neutral chip, no mark), unknown or null source kind ("Source unclear"), invalid or offset-less `nextStartAt` ("No upcoming time listed"), negative or null cost ("Price not listed"), duplicate activity IDs, very long unbroken titles, `reasons` containing blanks or non-strings.
- **Source URLs are not rendered** on cards. The URL and check details belong on the detail screen.

## Remaining limitations

- English strings are inline; no i18n.
- The interested count is a plain number, not live. Whether it updates after a peer saves depends on G3's watcher.
- The "next time" uses G4's `nextStartAt` only; the number of other options is a simple `timeOptions.length - 1`, and it includes options that may already be in the past.
