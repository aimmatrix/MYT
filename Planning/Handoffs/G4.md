## Files and exports

- `code/src/domain/matching.ts` exports `rankActivities(input: RankingInput): RankingResult`, plus the named input, result and issue interfaces.
- `RankingInput` contains `profile: Profile | null`, `activities: readonly Activity[]`, explicit `nowMs`, and optional `ActivityFilters`.
- `RankingResult.items` is G1's `RankedActivity[]`. `issues` contains invalid schedule or clock diagnostics with activity and option IDs.

## Behaviour

- Excludes unavailable activities and those with no valid future time option. Invalid start or end instants are reported as issues; wholly past but valid schedules are simply omitted.
- Applies interest filters by exact ID. An explicit availability filter keeps only activities with a confirmed overlap among their valid future options. Unknown availability is not called a mismatch, but cannot satisfy that filter.
- Orders by shared interest, known availability overlap, known budget fit, earliest valid future instant, activity ID, then title. The rules use booleans rather than a compatibility score.
- Emits a reason only for a proved match. Estimated budget fits retain an estimate qualifier. Unknown cost or availability never produces a positive reason.
- Source status and sample profiles do not affect ranking or counts.
