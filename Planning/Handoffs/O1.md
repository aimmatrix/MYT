## Decisions that unblock wave 1

1. **Scope is unchanged.** It keeps the fixed organiser, an immutable `plan:<activityId>` and separate per-person interest, vote and attendance files.
2. **Withdraw and decline write a status; nothing is deleted.** Once a plan exists, interest and votes are read-only in the UI. "Going" does not require prior interest.
3. **Data paths are fixed** in Architecture §2. G1's `paths.ts` is the only path builder. Keys use T3's `id-<hex>` format.
4. **ID rules:**
   - Real users come from `ctx.currentUser.id`.
   - Samples are prefixed `sample:` and preview fixtures `preview:`.
   - Activity IDs are `[a-z0-9-]`, at most 60 characters.
   - IDs are at most 120 UTF-8 bytes.
5. **Interface shapes for G1** are in Architecture §6:
   - `MytFilePort`, `MytError` codes, `Loadable` with per-record `issues`, `ActionResult`, and `CreatePlanResult` with `existing`
   - G9 action state, routes, the view model and its callbacks
   - the presence payload and room `myt-v1`
6. **"Missing" is detected by listing the parent**, never by parsing error strings. The same goes for "already exists" after a rejected `overwrite: false` write.
7. **Preview is explicit only.** A live load error never falls back to preview data.
8. **T1 decoder adds a plan consistency check:** the plan's `startAt` and `endAt` must match its chosen option. This catches a cross-device field merge.
9. **G6 seeds `data/session.json`** with `organiserUserId: null`, plus both catalogs, so `data/` exists before first load. Time options are at least 7 days after the demo.
